How it works

Three steps, zero manual work

You already use Stripe Workflows to react to payment events. Writeback gives those workflows a new action: write the result into the system your team actually looks at.

01 · Trigger

Pick the payment event

Payment succeeded, failed, refunded, or disputed — any Workflows trigger works.

02 · Action

Add Writeback as a step

It shows up in your Workflow builder like any built-in action. Map the fields once.

03 · Write-back

Your record updates itself

Paid status, receipt URL, and reference ID land in your form or ticket — seconds after the charge.

Questions

Straight answers

Is this made by Stripe?

No. Writeback is built by Cali Live Play LLC on Stripe's app platform. Stripe is a trademark of Stripe, Inc. We're an independent developer — not affiliated with or endorsed by Stripe.

Where does it run?

Depending on the connector, the action runs either on Stripe's app runtime or on our infrastructure. Either way, your Stripe secrets never leave Stripe, and connector credentials are stored encrypted.

What do you need for a private install?

One short call: which payment event triggers it, which system gets updated, and which fields map where. Install happens the same day via the Stripe CLI.

Can you build a connector we need?

Yes — the REST/webhook mode covers most systems today, and purpose-built connectors are added by request.
