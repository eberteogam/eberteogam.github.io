§04 · Questions

Straight answers

Are quotes really fixed?

Yes. Scope, fee, timeline, and exclusions go into a Statement of Work before work starts. If the scope changes, the quote changes — in writing, before the work.

Do you hand everything over?

Yes — documentation and a walkthrough are part of every engagement. The goal is that your team operates it without us. Ongoing support is available separately if you want it.

What stacks do you work in?

SailPoint IdentityIQ and Identity Security Cloud, GitHub Actions, Docker, Linux/WSL2, and REST integrations across ServiceNow, Moveworks, and standard APIs.
